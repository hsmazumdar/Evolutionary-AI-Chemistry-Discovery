# V05 Statistical Analysis
Concept and direction: Himanshu Mazumdar, R&D Centre, Dharmsinh Desai University · Code developed with AI assistance.

Generated by `scripts/run_statistics.py` (tables copied from `results/statistics_tables.md`).
Unit = system (n = 9). Each system's value is the mean over its splits/orders. Paired one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank in
the hypothesised direction, Cohen's d_z, and a system-cluster bootstrap 95% CI of the mean paired difference (10,000
resamples). Holm correction across the 4 primary tests. Success = Holm p < 0.05 **and** gain ≥ 0.02.
H3 uses an intersection-union test: memory must beat each of the 5 controls, and the reported p is the maximum of the 5.
The smallest possible one-sided Wilcoxon p with n = 9 is 0.002, so power is limited for small effects. Even so, H1's
observed effect is large and in the *opposite* direction.

**Deviations from the protocol, disclosed:**
1. H2's model class was not pinned in the protocol, so both PU_RF (H2a) and EHP (H2b) are tested and Holm then covers 4 tests, not 3.
2. H3 is analysed with the system as the unit (mean over 3 orders) and the cluster bootstrap. The protocol said
   "system × order". Collapsing over orders is the conservative choice.
3. H3 arms use 3 PU bags per episode, against 5 in H1. The budget is identical across arms.

## Interpretation
- H1 is rejected in the hypothesised direction. The reverse direction (baseline > EHP) has d_z = +3.03 with 9 of 9 systems
  and p = 0.002, and this holds for every sensitivity variant except Protocol B. The Protocol B exception comes from the
  non-grouped inner selection described in V05_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md.
- H2a, H2b and H3 show no effect: all CIs include 0 and all point estimates are below 0.02.
- Phase 8 p-values are exploratory and uncorrected.
